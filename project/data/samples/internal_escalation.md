# Internal escalation guide

## When to escalate

Support leads escalate refund disputes above $2,000 to the finance on-call rotation. Escalations must include the order number, customer history, and the reason the standard policy does not apply.

## Goodwill credits

Support leads may grant a goodwill credit of up to $50 without further approval when a customer has had two or more delivery problems in the last 90 days.
