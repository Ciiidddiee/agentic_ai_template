# Warranty

## Coverage

All Acme electronics include a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear.

## Making a claim

To make a warranty claim, customers need the order number and a short description of the fault. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 14 days.

## Extended warranty

An extended warranty adds 24 months of coverage and can be bought within 30 days of the original purchase.
