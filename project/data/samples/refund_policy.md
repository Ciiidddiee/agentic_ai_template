# Refund policy

## Return window

Customers can return most items within 30 days of purchase for a refund to the original payment method. Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a refund, but may qualify for store credit at the manager's discretion.

## Opened items

Opened items can be returned within the same 30-day window. A 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund for opened items. Unopened items in original packaging receive a full refund.

## Large refunds

Refunds above $500 need approval from a store manager before they are issued. The customer is told the expected decision time, which is usually one business day.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the return is received. Gift card purchases are refunded as a new gift card.
