# Shipping policy

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $15 per order.

## Free shipping

Orders over $75 ship free with standard shipping. Express shipping is never free.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If a parcel is lost or arrives damaged, customers should report it within 7 days of the expected delivery date. Acme will send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping costs.
